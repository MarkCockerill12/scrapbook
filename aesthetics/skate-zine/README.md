# 18 · City Escape '01 (slug `skate-zine`)

Rebuilt 2026-09-28. The August review chose "SA2 City Escape + Tony Hawk's Pro Skater,
combined"; this time Mark said the page "just looks like a mess". So: one screen, one
skater, one two-minute run.

- **The level** is a real steep San Francisco street (Flickr CC), rushing past;
  other stages on the right are real City Escape screenshots (Sonic wiki), a real
  concrete bowl and the cable-car line.
- **The skater** is a real photo, matted. Shadow and Rouge (official renders) frame
  the title screen; Shadow grinds past mid-run. The title has a rack of real box art
  (THPS 2/3/Underground, SA2, Jet Set Radio, JSRF).
- **THPS rules:** K/H/F flips, G/C grinds, M manual, J grab — chain within 2.2s for a
  combo (repeats score less), the combo banks with LANDED ×N, mashing within 140ms
  bails. The special meter fills; SPACE lands THE 900. S-K-A-T-E letters drift
  through the run; goals tick off; 2:00 timer; best score persisted.
- **Secret tape** under the goals panel plays a sponsor-tape cut of City Escape.
- **Music:** generated 168 BPM pop-punk (power chords, eighth-note bass, snare on
  2 and 4).

The old ransom-note zine (Ransom.tsx) is gone.
