# 06 · Neon — NEON//CITY

Rebuilt 2026-09-28. Mark: *"this isn't what I wanted at all — I wanted a neon city and
cool computer UIs and roblox avatars and different things that make use of neon
colours."* The CSS stick-figure and colour wheel are gone.

**The city** is real night photography (Mong Kok, Shinjuku in the rain, Shibuya, Yau
Ma Tei, Kabukicho — Flickr CC via Openverse, `nc_city`), parallaxing with the cursor
under canvas rain. **The interface** is CITY.OS, a holographic HUD in one small palette
(magenta / cyan / acid / amber). **The avatars** are real Roblox renders (Roblox wiki
lead images, `nc_roblox`, `nc_roblox_item`) lit like they're standing in the street.

Fonts self-hosted: Orbitron, Share Tech Mono, Monoton, Neonderthaw, Tilt Neon.

| Element | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| District feeds | six live "CCTV" thumbnails; click one to jump the whole city there (glitch cut), persisted |
| Street | click to scan a building — ping + readout |
| SIGNAL | three generated synthwave stations (Night Drive / Neon Rain / Arcade Heart); the bars dance to the score's actual energy; play unmutes |
| AVATAR.EXE | Roblox lobby on a neon grid: pick an avatar, aura colour, equip Valkyrie Helm / Sparkle Fedora, emotes (dance / wave / jump / spin), chat bubbles, click your avatar to *oof* |
| SIGN.FORGE | type up to 18 chars, pick tube font + colour, HANG IT — it buzzes on the city (last 5 persisted) |
| ⚡ BLACKOUT | the city goes dark, signs die, then the grid hums back |
| Ambience | rain bed + neon transformer hum; ticker |
